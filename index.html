Tournament communications

SMS and text notifications

Registered SATX Sports participants may choose to receive transactional SMS/text notifications related to the events they enter. These messages may include court or field assignments, match readiness notices, schedule updates, and other event-operation information.

Consent is optional. Participants may complete event registration without agreeing to receive SMS messages. Message frequency varies by event. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

SATX Sports does not use tournament text notifications for third-party marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
