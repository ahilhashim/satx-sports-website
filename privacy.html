Effective date: October 9, 2026

SATX Sports LLC (“SATX Sports,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information in connection with our sports tournaments, registrations, event operations, and communications.

We may collect information you provide during event registration, including your name, email address, mobile phone number, date of birth, team or partner information, emergency contact information, payment-related status, and event preferences.

How we use information

We use information to administer registrations, organize teams and schedules, operate events, communicate event information, process or verify payments, respond to questions, and maintain event records.

Mobile information and SMS consent

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

If you opt in to SATX Sports text notifications, message frequency varies by event. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Service providers

We may use service providers that help us operate registrations, payments, scheduling, communications, and event administration. These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to SATX Sports.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of registering for or participating in an event. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
